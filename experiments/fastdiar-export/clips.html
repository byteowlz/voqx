    Matching settings (exploratory)

    A name is shown only when the best speaker clears the match threshold, leads the runner-up by the margin, and stays the same for the stable time. Defaults come from a small pilot, not a qualified operating point. Changes apply instantly to everything already analysed. Speech detection uses Silero VAD, the same pinned model as eaRS: a window is only compared with your speakers when enough of it is speech, so silence, music and noise are skipped. Raise “Speech in window” to skip more speaker changes and pauses.

    Experimental. Scores are cosine similarities, not probabilities. Overlapping voices and voices not in your list can be mislabelled or shown as unknown. A recording of an enrolled voice can match. This is not authentication or permission.

    Silero VAD (as in eaRS) and FASTDIAR-small encoder on WASM CPU, one thread each · matching and stabilisation by the voqx Rust/WASM library · nothing persisted.
